We Were Made For This Moment

The abundance of change · A poem by Alfredo Mathew III

I was made for this moment.

I don’t mean to say this in a prideful way.

I mean, I am a runon sentence, I love spontaneity, I love making transitions an art.

Because I love change.

My favorite t-shirt in my twenties was a green t-shirt that said, “A Constant State of Change”.

I taught US History for 10 years in 3 high schools across 3 cities, in the Bronx, Pasadena and Oakland, but never the same lesson twice, and that includes in the same day. I loved the experience of watching a lesson evolve from 1st period to 5th as students unlocked the lesson inside the lesson.

I was made for adaptation, because history does not have one interpretation or one point of view.

I was made for this moment, because I am no longer afraid of you, what you think of me, what you think of yourself.

I used to care. I thought I had to prove my worth to be loved, that somehow, my best was never enough.

I had to die, to submit my life to God, to surrender everything, to realize I am loved and we are all going to be okay, because we are literally blessed with life in an otherwise empty universe.

We have a purpose. We make meaning together. We all BELONG!

I was made for this moment, because this is a decade of change. This is our Great Depression and WWII, our Civil Rights and Vietnam. We will never be the same, and we shouldn’t want to.

Because moments are opportunities for transformation. You should never let a good crisis go to waste. Buffaloes know you need to run towards an approaching storm. It’s painful at first, like a cold shower, then it sets you free. The gift of embracing change is Abundance.

We are holding onto the status quo, because we are holding on to… status.

We care about silly temporal things like the self, when there is a higher purpose and a deeper love moving the world forward. It is in your heart and it’s the light in the darkness.

I was made for this moment, because in the darkest room in the darkest hour, I always see the light. It is beautiful and it is awe inspiring. It is love. It is mine and it is yours.

My daughter’s name is Tara Luna, Star in Hindi / Moon in Spanish, she is half her mother and half her father, la luz en la oscuridad, the light shining in the darkness.

We were made for this moment. We were made for love.

By Alfredo Mathew III
Kissel Uptown Oakland
6/19/2024

This poem is dedicated to the spirit of my dear brother and mentor Steve Jubb on the night of his dear wife, Connie Jubb’s, 75th birthday.
